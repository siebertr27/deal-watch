How penny shopping works: a "penny item" is discontinued stock the store's system has dropped to $0.01. It rings up for a penny at the register even though the shelf tag still shows the old price — and stores are supposed to pull it, so a cashier can decline. Scan the barcode (Dollar General app, or the register) before counting on it. A sighting is historical: it tells you what to hunt, not that it's still there.

Walmart — clearance & hidden markdowns

Walmart runs clearance, not pennies. Markdowns show as yellow "reduced" stickers, and unadvertised hidden clearance can ring at 1¢–$3 when the barcode scans lower than the tag. There is no official list: it's a hunt. The free route is the Walmart app — open any clearance sticker's barcode scanner to see the real price before you commit.

Start at walmart.com's clearance search. The Tue/Fri watch checks Walmart and Target clearance alongside the penny lists from the next run.
